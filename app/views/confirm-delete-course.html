Manage an apprenticeship

Are you sure you want to remove this apprenticeship?

Removing an apprenticeship will remove it from your account. It will no longer show on Find apprenticeship training.

You will also be unable to claim funding for new learners on the course.

However, you can still submit Individualised Learning Records (ILRs) for learners already on the course.
