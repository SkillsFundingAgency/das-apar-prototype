What do the different statuses mean?

Not available yet

This means the course can be advertised on Find apprenticeship training, but you can’t enrol learners or claim government funding yet.

When it becomes available, its status will change to open to new starts.

Open to new starts

This delivery status appears on courses you currently offer and advertise on Find apprenticeship training.

You can claim government funding for them by submitting Individualised Learning Records (ILRs).

Last start date added

The Department for Education (DfE) will add a last date for new starts to courses that will not be available in the future.

You can continue to enrol new government funded learners up until the last start date.

Closed to new starts

This means the last date for new starts set by DfE has now passed.

If your course is closed to new starts, you will not be able to enrol new learners or claim government funding.

You can continue to train learners who are already on the course.
